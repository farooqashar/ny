About NYC

New York City is the most populous city in the United States, with over 8 million residents across five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx, and Staten Island.

Known for its cultural diversity, world-class dining, Broadway theaters, and towering skyscrapers, NYC is a global hub for finance, media, art, fashion, and entertainment.
